Terms of service

Last updated 9 May 2026

Our portfolio ranges from technical advisory to field execution and trading of capital equipment. These terms apply unless an executed master services agreement explicitly supersedes them.

Scope of engagement

Work only begins after a signed technical proposal and written order. Any extra scope must be priced and approved before mobilization.

Intellectual property

CAD models, UAV orthomosaics, proprietary calculation notes and similar deliverables remain UPUW property until associated milestone invoices are settled. After payment you receive a non-exclusive license unless otherwise stated.

Liability & compliance

  • HSE plans approved before each site transition.
  • Customs and excise compliance for imported specialty equipment.
  • Force majeure covers climate, grid or logistics shocks outside our reasonable control.

Privacy policy

Data protection protocol

We collect only data required to deliver your contract, invoice correctly or answer statutory requests. Hosting may occur inside or outside the EU with appropriate SCC or equivalent safeguards.

Data collection

  • Project artifacts: drawings, UAV logs, approximate geolocation metadata.
  • Communications: corporate email and professional WhatsApp threads you initiate.
  • Analytics: aggregated server logs — no third-party ad profiling.

Data utilization

Information powers delivery planning, warranty support and internal process improvement. We never sell identifiable datasets. Retention follows Cameroonian limitation periods plus any cross-border civil duties.

Billing & refund policy

Financial protocols

Long-horizon programs lock vendor capacity the moment we commit steel, fiberglass or air freight. The rules below balance buyer protection with supplier reality.

Initial retainers

Unless UPUW is solely responsible for a demonstrable delay or illegality, mobilization deposits are non-refundable after official kickoff.

Milestone billing

Each invoice maps to a verifiable deliverable or physical state. Disputes must be filed within seven (7) days of formal receipt or acceptance is implied.

Early termination reconciles only completed milestones net of unavoidable sunk costs (equipment, dedicated personnel, repositioned travel). No credit may exceed the unpaid balance minus documented irreversible spend, evidenced within eight (8) calendar days.
